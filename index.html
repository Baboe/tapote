Made here

Printed 0.2 mm at a time

A backpack tag is 5 mm thick. That is 25 layers of PETG, laid down one by one in our workshop in the Dordogne. Then the chip goes into its pocket and the tag is sealed.

The chip is an NTAG213 with 144 bytes of memory, enough for one short link. It has no battery. The phone powers it for the half second it needs.

  • No app.
  • No battery.
  • No subscription.
  • No GPS.

A Tapote object does one thing. Someone has to hold a phone to it, and then it opens a page. It can't track anything, and we won't claim it can.
